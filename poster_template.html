Problem Definition

  • Traditional comic creation demands immense artistic skill, time, and financial resources.
  • Existing generative AI tools struggle with character consistency across multiple scenes.
  • Current diffusion models suffer from concept bleeding when generating multiple distinct characters in a single panel.

Objectives

  • Zero-Shot Identity: Use PuLID & IP-Adapter for biometric character anchoring — no model training required.
  • Spatial Coherence: Leverage Flux.1 Transformer to eliminate concept bleeding in multi-character panels.
  • Unified Workspace: React/Konva drag-and-drop canvas for layout assembly and speech bubble typesetting.

Architecture

A decoupled pipeline: Next.js frontend → FastAPI backend → headless ComfyUI on Colab GPU.
